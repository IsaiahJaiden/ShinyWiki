# Voting Rewards

Voting for ShinyVale on the server-list sites takes ten seconds and actually pays off — every vote gives you a reward, and keeping it up earns you extra.

## How to vote

```
/vote
```

This shows you the list of voting sites and links to vote on each one. Most sites let you vote again every 24 hours.

<div class="sv-cmd-strip" markdown>
`/vote` `/votetop` `/votestreak` `/votetotal`
</div>

## What you get

**Every single vote** gives you a flat reward, on top of anything below.

<div class="sv-grid" markdown>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-star: Milestones</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Hit 20 lifetime votes and you'll get a one-time bonus reward on top of your normal vote reward.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-fire: Voting Streaks</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Vote on consecutive days, weeks, or months and earn extra rewards for keeping the streak alive — the longer the streak, the bigger the bonus.</span>
</div>

</div>

!!! tip "Bonuses stack"
    A single vote can trigger your normal reward **and** a milestone **and** a streak bonus all at once if you happen to cross more than one threshold at the same time — you're never shortchanged for good timing.

## Checking your progress

- `/votestreak` — see your current daily, weekly, and monthly streaks, plus your all-time best for each.
- `/votetotal` — see your total vote count (this is what counts toward milestones).
- `/votetop` — see the server's top voters.

!!! info "Offline voting counts"
    You don't have to be online for a vote to count — voting while offline still adds to your total and keeps your streak alive.
