# Calendar & Rewards

ShinyVale runs a daily reward calendar — log in, open it, and claim that day's present. Miss a day? You can still go back and grab it later.

## Opening the calendar

```
/calendar
```

Aliases: `/axcalendar`, `/adventcalendar`, `/axadventcalendar` — all open the same menu.

This opens a book-shaped grid where each numbered day is its own claimable present. Presents look different depending on their state:

!!! tip "Late claiming is allowed"
    Been away for a few days? ShinyVale lets you claim older, unclaimed presents late — you don't lose them just because you missed a day.

## What you can get

Rewards escalate the further into the month you get, mixing cosmetic items, ShinyVale-flavored loot, and real economy rewards:

- **Money** — every day includes a cash reward that scales up as the days go on.
- **Themed items** — flavor items tied to other ShinyVale systems (the Duels arena, RTP, claims, the shop, warps, and more), plus a few purely cosmetic pieces.
- **Crate keys and shards** — several days include a bonus crate key or a handful of shards on top of the usual reward, working tier by tier from Common up toward Crimson as the days progress.

The final day of the cycle is the biggest reward of the month — worth making sure you don't miss it.

!!! info "One claim per day, per account"
    ShinyVale limits how many accounts on the same IP can claim a given day's reward, so this isn't something to farm with alt accounts.
