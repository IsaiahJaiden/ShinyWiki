# Auction House

The auction house is a shared marketplace where any player can list an item for other players to buy directly — separate from your own [player shop](economy.md#player-owned-shops).

## Opening it

```
/ah
```

Aliases: `/auction`, `/auctions`, `/crazyauctions`, `/crazyauction`, `/ca` — all open the same menu.

## Listing an item

Hold the item you want to sell, then run:

```
/ah sell <price>
```

Your listing goes live for other players to browse and buy at that fixed price.

!!! info "Fixed-price only"
    Bidding isn't currently active — every listing is buy-it-now at whatever price you set. There's no back-and-forth auction happening, just a straightforward listing.

## How long listings last

A listing stays active for a day before it's pulled from the browsable list, and there's a longer window after that before an unsold item fully expires — so there's no rush the moment you list something, but don't forget to check back and collect unsold items or your earnings.
