# Bounties

Got beef with another player, or just want to reward whoever takes down the server's biggest troublemaker? Bounties let you put real money on a player's head for anyone to collect.

## Placing a bounty

Run `/bounty <player> <amount>` to add money to someone's bounty. You'll get a confirmation screen showing exactly who you're targeting and how much you're putting up before it's final — nothing happens until you click **Confirm**.

!!! tip "Notifications"
    The target gets notified when someone adds to their bounty. If you'd rather not see those pop-ups yourself, toggle them off with `/bountytoggle`.

## Full command reference

See [Duels & Bounties commands](../commands/duels-bounties.md) for the complete list with syntax and examples.

!!! info "How payout works"
    Bounties are meant to be settled through the server's normal PvP systems — see [Duels & PvP](duels.md). Exact payout conditions can change, so if you're unsure whether a specific kill will settle a bounty, ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/G74PkPv2XQ) or check with staff.

## Related rules

Bounties are a real-money system tied to other players — the normal [server rules](../rules/index.md) around harassment still apply. Placing a bounty is fine; using the system to specifically target and harass one player over and over is not.
