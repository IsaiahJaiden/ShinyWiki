# Server Rules

These are the same rules shown in-game via `/rules`. Breaking them can result in a warning, mute, kick, or ban depending on severity — staff make that call, not this page.

!!! info "Where these come from"
    This page mirrors ShinyVale's live in-game rule book exactly. If it's ever out of date, `/rules` in-game is always the source of truth.

---

## General Conduct

No exploiting the game or the server in ways that give you an unfair advantage.

<div class="sv-grid" markdown>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-robot-off-outline: No Hacked Clients</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Movement mods, inventory mods, health indicators, radar, freecam, and auto-place are all banned. Vanilla client only.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-console: No Macros or Scripts</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">No harmful macros or automation scripts of any kind.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-bug: No Abusing Bugs</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Found a bug or dupe method? Report it — don't use it. See [Reporting](../help/reporting.md).</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-cash-remove: No IRL Trading</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">No real-money trading, no cross-server trading, and no invite-for-reward schemes.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-link-off: No Sharing Links</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Don't post outside links in chat.</span>
</div>

</div>

## Claim Rules

ShinyVale's [claim system](../gameplay/claims.md) exists so you can protect your builds — not so it can be turned into a weapon against other players.

!!! warning "Don't weaponize claiming"
    You may **not** use `/claim` to troll, grief, or harass another player. That includes claiming land purely to block, obstruct, or otherwise interfere with someone else's legitimate claim.

<div class="sv-grid" markdown>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-emoticon-angry-outline: No Claiming to Be a Menace</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Placing a claim purely to troll, block, or interfere with someone else — rather than to protect your own build — isn't allowed.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-shield-off: No Griefing Others' Claims</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Don't abuse claim mechanics to mess with a claim that legitimately belongs to someone else.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-check-circle: Normal Claiming Is Always Fine</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Claiming land to protect your own base, farm, or project is exactly what the system is for. Go for it.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-gavel: Staff Have Final Say</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Whether a specific claim is legitimate or abusive is a judgment call staff make case by case.</span>
</div>

</div>
