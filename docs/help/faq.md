# FAQ

**How do I protect my base?**
Use `/claim` — see the full [Claims guide](../gameplay/claims.md).

**How do I get money?**
Sell items with `/sell`, complete bulk orders with `/order`, or trade at the [auction house](../gameplay/auctions.md). See [Economy & Shops](../gameplay/economy.md).

**How do I find somewhere to build?**
Run `/rtp` to teleport somewhere random, then claim it once you've picked a spot.

**How do I get back to spawn?**
`/spawn`. To get back to a specific base you've built, set a home there with `/sethome` and use `/home` afterward.

**How do I fight another player fairly?**
Challenge them with `/duel <player>`, or queue up with `/queue`. See [Duels & PvP](../gameplay/duels.md).

**How do I put a bounty on someone?**
`/bounty <player> <amount>`. See [Bounties](../gameplay/bounties.md).

**What's the daily reward thing?**
That's the calendar — run `/calendar` to claim it. See [Calendar & Rewards](../features/calendar.md).

**Does voting actually do anything?**
Yes — every vote gives a real reward, plus bonus rewards for voting streaks and lifetime milestones. See [Voting Rewards](../features/voting.md).

**The resource pack won't load — what do I do?**
See [Resource Pack](../features/resource-pack.md#if-it-doesnt-load) for the fix.

**I think someone is cheating/breaking the rules — what do I do?**
See [Reporting a Player](reporting.md).

**Can I play on console/mobile?**
Yes — see [Bedrock & Crossplay](../features/bedrock.md).

**I died — did I lose everything?**
Your items drop into a grave at your death location instead of scattering — head back and grab it before it expires.

**Can I trade directly with another player?**
Yes — approach them in-world to start a direct trade with a confirmation screen on both sides. See [Social Commands](../commands/social.md).
