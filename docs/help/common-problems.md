# Common Problems

## Resource pack won't load

See the dedicated [Resource Pack troubleshooting section](../features/resource-pack.md#if-it-doesnt-load) — the short version: rejoin, and make sure Server Resource Packs is set to Enabled in your Minecraft options.

## I can't build here

You've probably wandered into someone else's [claim](../gameplay/claims.md). Move on, or ask the claim owner to trust you with `/claim add`.

## My `/rtp` isn't working

There's a short cooldown between random teleports — if you just used it, wait a bit and try again.

## I got a teleport request I didn't want

Deny it with `/tpdeny`, or turn off incoming requests entirely with `/tptoggle`.

## I'm not getting a voting reward

Double check you're voting through `/vote`'s links — rewards are tied to those specific sites. If a vote genuinely isn't crediting after a few minutes, mention it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/G74PkPv2XQ).

## Still stuck?

Ask in the [ShinyVale Discord](https://discord.gg/G74PkPv2XQ) — that's the fastest way to reach staff and other players.
