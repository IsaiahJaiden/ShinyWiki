# Reporting a Player

Found a cheater, a bug, or someone breaking the [rules](../rules/index.md)? Here's how to flag it to staff.

## Report it in Discord

The official way to report bugs, glitches, and cheaters is in the **[ShinyVale Discord](https://discord.gg/G74PkPv2XQ)**. Include as much detail as you can:

- The player's exact username
- What happened and roughly when
- Screenshots or a clip if you have one — this makes a huge difference

## What to expect

Staff review reports and act on confirmed rule-breaking — bans, mutes, and other actions are a staff judgment call based on the evidence, not something that happens automatically.

!!! warning "Don't take matters into your own hands"
    Retaliating against a rule-breaker (griefing back, harassing them, exploiting the same bug) breaks the rules just as much as what they did. Report it and let staff handle it.
