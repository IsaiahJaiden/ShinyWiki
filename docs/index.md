## Why ShinyVale?

<div class="sv-grid" markdown>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-eye-outline: 32-Chunk View Distance</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">See the world the way big builds deserve to be seen. Distant terrain stays visible instead of vanishing into fog, exploration feels more rewarding, and you're not stuck squinting at a tiny multiplayer render distance.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-earth: 30M × 30M World</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">A genuinely massive survival world — 30,000,000 × 30,000,000 blocks. Plenty of room to spread out, build far from spawn, and never feel crowded, no matter how long you play.</span>
</div>

<div class="sv-card" markdown>
<span class="sv-card-title">:material-cube-outline: Vanilla at Heart</span>
<span class="sv-card-desc">Redstone, farms, and core survival mechanics behave the way you expect. ShinyVale adds claims, an economy, shops, and events <em>around</em> Minecraft — it doesn't replace the game underneath them.</span>
</div>

</div>
